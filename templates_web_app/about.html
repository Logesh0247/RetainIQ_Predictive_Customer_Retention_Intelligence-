About RetainIQ

Customer Churn Prediction & Retention Intelligence Platform

Purpose

Customer churn — when a subscriber cancels or stops using a service — is one of the most expensive problems in telecom. RetainIQ uses machine learning to predict which customers are likely to churn before they leave, so retention teams can act early rather than reactively.

Machine Learning

RetainIQ supports and was benchmarked against the following model families:

  • Logistic Regression
  • Random Forest
  • XGBoost
  • LightGBM
